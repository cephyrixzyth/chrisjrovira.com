Carrying a theme into the browser tab

Portfolio · Change published October 4, 2026

Changing the theme once updated the document’s theme color while leaving the tab icon fixed. The October 4 revision extended the same selection to the favicon.

What changed

The theme handler reads the active background and accent colors and uses them to construct the small CJR icon. The tab can now carry the palette someone chose on the page. Open the homepage, choose Forest and inspect the tab.
